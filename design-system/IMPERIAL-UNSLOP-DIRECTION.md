# Imperial Novel: Unslop design direction

Status: project-specific design authority for screen-level audits and refinements.

## Direction
- Product: original reading, discovery and publishing platform for readers and creators.
- Character: imperial, literary, cultivated, futuristic.
- World: celestial imperial library, black-sun architecture, open gates, botanical gardens, natural landscapes and contemporary luxury editorial craft.
- Signature: black-sun crest and open-gate motif.

## Color architecture
| Role | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Warm reading paper | #FDFBF7 | Long-form reading |
| Pure white | #FFFFFF | Light theme surfaces |
| Obsidian | #0B1215 | Primary dark theme ground |
| Midnight | #05070C | Deepest theme |
| Raised dark surface | #151F24 | Dark theme cards and panels |
| Imperial purple | #6A0DAD | Primary brand accent |
| Sapphire | #0F52BA | Discovery and selected navigation |
| Emerald | #087F5B | Positive/organic semantic accent |
| Restrained gold | #C5A253 | Rare premium detail, not body text by default |

The wider purple family (Tyrian, royal, amethyst, electric, galaxy, cosmic, lavender, plum and Byzantine) is available for artwork, covers and deliberate brand moments. Do not use every shade on one screen. UI chrome stays calm; color-rich moments must have product meaning.

## Quality rules
1. No generic AI dashboard or template composition.
2. No decorative purple-blue gradients, gradient text, neon glows, glass fog or ornamental blobs.
3. Use original literary/editorial composition and actual product content.
4. Keep reading typography and surfaces comfortable for extended sessions.
5. Preserve the established black-sun crest and open-gate signature.
6. Nature and fashion imagery must reinforce a real content or brand purpose.
7. Preserve RTL, responsive behavior, WCAG AA and low-end Android performance.
8. Do not invent content, functionality, metrics, testimonials or rights claims.

## Unslop quality gate
Audit first, then work in this order: spacing, color, icons, copy, typography, layout, details, visuals and motion. Verify with a rendered screen where possible. A successful build alone is not visual verification.
