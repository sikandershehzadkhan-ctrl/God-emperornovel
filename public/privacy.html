Privacy at Imperial Novel

We use the information needed to operate a reading and creator platform, protect accounts, keep reading progress, process ticket requests, and moderate content. We aim to collect only what is useful for those purposes.

Do not send passwords, payment PINs, identity documents, or other sensitive information through public comments or support messages.

Information we handle

  • Account details and session/security data needed to sign in and protect your account.
  • Reading progress, bookmarks, library choices, ratings, comments, and follows that support your experience.
  • Ticket ledger entries, payment references, and manual review states for ticket purchases or support requests.
  • Novel metadata, creator submissions, rights evidence, reports, and moderation decisions.
  • Author applications, including a public author name, biography, optional experience and portfolio link, preferred formats, a writing sample, rights confirmation, review feedback, and the application decision.
  • Basic operational and security signals used to diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and keep the service reliable.

Creator applications

Author applications are visible to administrators responsible for creator review. The writing sample is collected to assess an application and must be original or authorized for submission. Application details and decisions may be retained to administer creator access, prevent repeated abuse, preserve review history, and address legal or security issues. Applicants can check their status and any review feedback in Author Studio.

Payments and tickets

Where payment confirmation is handled manually, a request remains pending until an authorized administrator reviews it. We do not treat a submitted receipt as proof of approval. Never share your EasyPaisa PIN or one-time code with Imperial Novel staff.

Connected sources and AI

External connectors must be authorized before activation. Discovery workflows should use permitted metadata and source links, not copy chapter bodies without rights. AI features should only send information to a configured provider when the feature is enabled and the request requires it.

Your choices and account safety

Use account controls to manage your profile and reading experience. Keep your credentials private, sign out on shared devices, and report suspicious access or content through the available platform controls. Some records may need to be retained to protect security, settle ticket disputes, or meet legal obligations.

Contact and updates

Privacy and security practices may evolve as the platform grows. Material changes should be reflected on this page. For help, use the platform's available support and reporting routes.
