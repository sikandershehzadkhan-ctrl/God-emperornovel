RIGHTS FIRST · CREATOR RESPECT

Rights & Copyright

Imperial Novel is intended for original, licensed, authorized, public-domain, and appropriately licensed community works. Discovery must never be a shortcut around permission.

What may be published

  • Original stories and artwork created by the submitting creator.
  • Works covered by a valid license or explicit authorization, with required attribution and terms respected.
  • Public-domain works after checking the applicable jurisdiction and edition.
  • Creative Commons works only when the specific license permits the intended use and all conditions are met.
  • Fanfiction only where the relevant rights holder's rules and applicable law permit it.

Evidence and review

Creators and source partners may be asked to provide an evidence URL, license terms, attribution, and notes explaining the permitted use. Content can remain in draft or pending review until an authorized reviewer confirms the rights basis. An imported link or metadata record is not proof that chapter text may be copied.

Metadata-first discovery

Trend Factory may help identify public metadata signals and potential authors to invite. It must not ingest or republish chapter bodies without documented rights. OmniPortal providers stay disabled until permissions are approved.

Report a concern

If you believe material infringes your rights, provide the exact Imperial Novel page, identify the protected work, explain your relationship to the rights holder, and include reliable contact details and supporting evidence. Do not submit false or knowingly misleading reports. Content may be restricted while a good-faith report is reviewed.
