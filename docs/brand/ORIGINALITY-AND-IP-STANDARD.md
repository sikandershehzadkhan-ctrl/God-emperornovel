# Imperial Novel: Originality and Intellectual Property Standard

- **Status:** Product-wide mandatory standard
- **Applies to:** Brand, design, frontend, backend, content, media, community, creator tools, marketing, and operations
- **Product:** Imperial Novel
- **Motto:** Read • Create • Conquer

## 1. Non-negotiable principle

Imperial Novel is an original product. We do not clone, reskin, trace, or reproduce another publisher, reading platform, game, anime, template, or software product. The platform's identity and distinctive expression must be conceived and developed for Imperial Novel.

A competitor may be studied to understand a user problem or common industry convention. Research is not permission to copy its protected expression. Translate useful observations into independently reasoned product decisions and original implementations.

## 2. What must be original

Create and maintain original work for:

- Brand identity: name presentation, crest, logo system, symbols, visual motifs, brand voice, and messaging.
- Visual design: page composition, navigation, typography hierarchy, spacing, color application, cards, controls, transitions, and responsive behavior.
- Product experiences: homepage, discovery, catalog, reader, author studio, profiles, community, administration, and all workflows.
- Creative assets: illustrations, icons, textures, backgrounds, animations, empty states, and promotional artwork.
- Written expression: interface copy, help text, onboarding, policy explanations, marketing copy, and documentation.
- Software: application-specific source code, business logic, schemas, APIs, and original components.

Use the established Imperial Novel direction as a starting point, not as a limitation: modern imperial authority, literary depth, celestial architecture, restrained luxury, and comfortable reading. Every resulting design must be purposefully composed for this product.

## 3. Inspiration versus copying

Allowed:
- Studying public product flows to identify user needs and usability conventions.
- Using commonplace interaction patterns such as search, pagination, tabs, bookmarks, and accessible form controls.
- Learning general engineering techniques from public documentation and open-source projects.
- Creating a new solution from a written problem statement and independently defined requirements.

Not allowed:
- Reproducing a competitor's page or component arrangement closely enough to function as a clone.
- Copying proprietary source code, text, illustrations, icons, branding, or distinctive assets without permission.
- Tracing screenshots or prompting an image model to recreate a named competitor's protected design.
- Removing attribution or licence notices, bypassing licence restrictions, or presenting third-party work as wholly original.
- Using another platform's content, covers, chapter text, artwork, or datasets without documented rights.

When a design reference is used, record what general problem or principle was learned and document how the Imperial Novel solution differs.

## 4. Third-party software and assets

"Original product" does not mean every dependency must be reinvented. Secure, maintained open-source libraries can be used when their licences permit the intended use.

Before adoption:
1. Identify the exact package, version, author, source, and licence.
2. Check licence obligations, attribution, notices, redistribution terms, and any copyleft implications.
3. Record the dependency in the repository's licence inventory.
4. Prefer small, maintained, auditable dependencies with no paid runtime requirement.
5. Do not copy a library's demo branding or sample product identity into Imperial Novel.
6. Keep required copyright and licence notices intact.

For fonts, photographs, illustrations, sound, video, and other media, record provenance and usage rights. Do not assume that something found online or labelled "free" is unrestricted.

## 5. Ownership boundaries

Keep these categories distinct:

- **Imperial Novel-authored work:** Original code, brand materials, interface copy, and creative assets should have documented authorship and ownership/assignment appropriate to the actual legal owner.
- **Open-source and third-party work:** Remains subject to its respective copyright and licence. It must never be described as exclusively owned by Imperial Novel.
- **Creator submissions:** Stories, chapters, covers, comics, illustrations, and other creator material remain the creator's property unless a separate, informed written agreement explicitly provides otherwise. Platform hosting or display permission must be scoped and recorded.
- **User contributions:** Comments, reviews, profile content, and uploads must be governed by clear terms and moderation rules; do not silently claim ownership of user content.
- **Public-domain or licensed material:** May be used only within the documented scope and conditions of its rights status or licence.

Do not promise that an asset is copyright-clear, trademark-clear, or exclusively owned until provenance and legal review support that claim.

## 6. Original creation workflow

For every substantial feature or visual system:
1. Start with the Imperial Novel user problem, not a competitor screenshot.
2. Write original requirements and acceptance criteria.
3. Define a distinct interaction and visual concept.
4. Build from the Imperial Novel design tokens and component rules.
5. Create or commission original assets, or select properly licensed assets.
6. Record sources, licences, authorship, and any permissions.
7. Review for accidental similarity, copied text, unlicensed assets, and confusingly similar branding.
8. Test accessibility, mobile usability, performance, and consistency.
9. Preserve design decisions and implementation history in version control.

## 7. Brand direction

The brand foundation remains:

- **Name:** Imperial Novel
- **Motto:** Read • Create • Conquer
- **Core character:** Modern imperial authority, literary imagination, confidence, clarity, and reader comfort.
- **Visual vocabulary:** A distinctive imperial celestial identity, developed through original geometry, symbols, compositions, and art direction.
- **Color foundation:** Obsidian, soft reading whites, Imperial Purple, and Sapphire Blue, applied with restraint and accessible contrast.

The crest, logo, type system, illustrations, and signature layouts must be newly designed for Imperial Novel. Color choices alone do not make a design original.

## 8. Enforcement and release gate

A feature or release must be held for correction if it contains:
- An unlicensed or untraceable asset.
- Copied competitor text, art, code, or distinctive interface expression.
- Missing required third-party notices or licence compliance.
- Misleading claims that creator or third-party work belongs exclusively to Imperial Novel.
- A brand treatment likely to confuse users with another organization.

Maintain an asset and dependency provenance record. When ownership or permission is uncertain, do not publish the material until it is resolved.

## 9. Honest intellectual-property claims

This standard is a product and engineering policy, not a legal opinion or a declaration that every element is already owned by Imperial Novel. Copyright, trademark, licence, employment/contractor assignment, and user-content rules depend on jurisdiction and the actual agreements. Obtain qualified legal review before making formal exclusivity, registration, or infringement claims.

**The goal:** an unmistakably Imperial Novel experience, built from independently created work, with transparent respect for the rights of creators and third-party authors.
