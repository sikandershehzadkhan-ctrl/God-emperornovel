The Weight of a Crown

The city woke beneath a quiet sky. Beyond the eastern towers, the first light moved across the old stone roofs, touching each window without demanding attention.

For a moment, the palace was still. The archive doors stood open, and the long gallery carried the scent of paper, cedar and rain. Every page held a decision someone had once believed impossible.

Power is not measured by how brightly a throne shines, but by how carefully its keeper listens.

He turned another page. The ink had faded, yet the meaning remained clear. A kingdom could survive a thousand storms and still be undone by a single careless choice.
